Delivery Information

Shipping Policy

We are committed to delivering every Scentora fragrance with care, precision, and the attention to detail expected from a luxury experience.

01

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. During promotional periods, holidays, or high-demand seasons, processing times may be slightly longer.

02

Domestic Shipping

Standard delivery within India generally takes 3–7 business days after dispatch. Delivery times may vary depending on location and courier service availability.

03

International Shipping

International orders are expected to arrive within 7–14 business days after dispatch. Customs procedures, import regulations, and local delivery networks may affect delivery times.

04

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation email containing tracking information, allowing you to monitor the progress of your shipment.

05

Shipping Charges

Shipping fees are calculated during checkout based on delivery location and selected shipping method. Any applicable charges will be clearly displayed before payment.

06

Delivery Delays

While we strive to meet estimated delivery timelines, unforeseen circumstances such as severe weather, transportation disruptions, customs inspections, or courier-related issues may occasionally cause delays.
